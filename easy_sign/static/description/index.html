🔒

100% Self-Hosted — Your Documents Never Leave Your Server

Unlike modules that depend on DocuSign, iLoveSign, or HelloSign, Easy Sign works entirely inside Odoo. No API keys, no third-party accounts, no monthly fees — and your documents are never sent to external servers.

Features

Everything You Need to Sign Documents

A complete signing workflow — upload, send, sign, and download — all inside Odoo.

📄

PDF Document Upload

Upload any PDF and add one or multiple signers with name and email. Set expiry dates and custom invitation messages.

📧

Automatic Email Invitations

Each signer receives a beautiful branded email with a unique, secure signing link. No Odoo account needed to sign.

✍️

Canvas Signature Drawing

Signers draw their signature directly in the browser using mouse or finger on any smartphone or tablet.

📋

Signed PDF Generation

After all parties sign, signatures are automatically embedded into the original PDF and saved in Odoo.

🔍

Full Audit Trail

Every action — viewed, signed, declined — is logged with IP address, timestamp, and signer details for legal compliance.

🌐

Arabic & English — RTL Support

The signing page switches instantly between Arabic (RTL) and English with a single click. Cairo font and full RTL layout included.

Workflow

How It Works in 4 Steps

1

Upload & Configure

Upload PDF, add signers, set expiry date and invitation message.

2

Send Invitations

Click "Send" — each signer receives a secure email with unique signing link.

3

Review & Sign

Signer opens link, views PDF, draws signature on canvas, clicks Sign.

✓

Signed PDF Ready

Signature embedded in PDF, saved in Odoo, requester notified automatically.
