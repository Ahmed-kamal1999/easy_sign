100% Self-Hosted — Your Documents Stay on Your Server

Unlike modules that depend on DocuSign, iLoveSign, or HelloSign, Easy Sign works entirely inside Odoo. No API keys, no third-party accounts, no subscription fees, and your documents are never sent to external servers.

Features

Everything You Need to Sign Documents

A complete signing workflow — from document upload to signed PDF — all inside Odoo.

📄

PDF Document Upload

Upload any PDF and add one or multiple signers with name and email. Set expiry dates and custom invitation messages.

📧

Automatic Email Invitations

Each signer receives a beautiful branded email with a unique, secure signing link. No Odoo account needed to sign.

✍️

Canvas Signature Drawing

Signers draw their signature directly in the browser using mouse or finger on any mobile device.

📋

Signed PDF Generation

After all parties sign, signatures are automatically embedded into the PDF and the signed document is saved in Odoo.

🔍

Full Audit Trail

Every action — viewed, signed, declined — is logged with IP address, timestamp, and signer details for legal compliance.

⏰

Expiry Dates & Decline

Set link expiry dates. Signers can decline with a reason, and the requester is notified immediately via Odoo chatter.

🌐

Arabic & English Interface

The signing page switches instantly between Arabic (RTL layout) and English with a single click — no page reload needed.

📱

Mobile Responsive

Fully optimized for smartphones and tablets. Touch-based signature drawing works perfectly on all mobile devices.

🔔

Smart Notifications

Requester is notified when all signers complete or when anyone declines. Integrated with Odoo chatter for full tracking.

Workflow

How It Works

Four simple steps from document upload to signed PDF.

1

Upload & Configure

Upload a PDF, add signers' names and emails, set optional expiry date and personal message.

2

Send Invitations

Click "Send for Signature" — Odoo emails each signer a secure, unique signing link instantly.

3

Review & Sign

Signer opens the link, reviews the PDF, draws signature on canvas, and clicks Sign.

4

Signed PDF Ready

Signatures embedded into PDF, saved in Odoo, requester notified automatically.
